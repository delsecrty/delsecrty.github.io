The problem

The customer you didn’t answer is already messaging someone else

When you’re up a ladder, hands in a pipe or polishing a car, you can’t answer WhatsApp. And when you finally get a moment at night, half the messages are the same old questions and the other half already hired whoever replied first.

An AI assistant handles that part for you: it replies instantly, at any hour, with your information and in your tone. It gathers what you need to quote, books the visit and only alerts you when something needs you.

You keep working. The assistant makes sure no inquiry goes unanswered.

Quotes

Areas you cover, hours, payment methods, warranty, materials, lead times. The things you answer twenty times a week.

03

Books visits

It offers your free slots, books the visit in your calendar and confirms day, time and address with the customer.

An emergency, a big client or something it can’t answer: it passes it to you right away, with a summary of the conversation, so you can call.

It separates real inquiries from browsers and collects name, area and type of job, so when you call you already know what it’s about.

06

Follows up

It messages customers who asked for a quote and went quiet, so you don’t lose jobs for lack of time.

FAQ

What independent professionals ask us

What is an AI virtual assistant?

It is a program that chats with your customers in writing, the way a receptionist would: it understands what they ask, answers with your information, asks for the details you need to quote and books visits. Unlike an automated answering system, it doesn’t follow a fixed menu: it understands questions written in many different ways.

Do I need an office, a computer or servers?

No. The assistant runs in the cloud and we handle the technical side. It is designed precisely for independent professionals who work on the road or on site and have no office.

Can it give quotes without me?

It can give an estimate using the price list you provide, and ask for measurements or photos to refine it. You confirm the final quote, or it remains subject to a visit. You decide how far the assistant goes and what stays with you.

What if a customer asks something the assistant doesn’t know?

It doesn’t make things up: it tells the customer it will check and forwards it to you with a summary of the conversation. Same with emergencies or big jobs: it alerts you right away so you can call.

Will my customers realize it’s a bot?

We set it up with your tone and your information, so it replies naturally. Still, we recommend it introduces itself as your assistant: customers value a fast, clear answer more than waiting hours for you to reply.

Does it work for my trade?

If you get customer inquiries —asking for prices, whether you cover their area or to schedule a time— yes. We work with plumbers, painters, electricians, builders, detailers and any trade or profession that depends on replying fast.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the features you need and the channels it has to cover. Tell us your trade and how inquiries reach you today, and we’ll send you a concrete, no-obligation proposal.
