FAQ

What companies ask us before implementing AI

What is private or on-premise AI?

It is an AI model that runs on infrastructure your company controls —your own servers or a dedicated private cloud— instead of a shared public service. The information it processes never leaves that environment, and you decide who has access, what gets logged and how long it is kept.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. In Del Security implementations your data is not shared with third parties or used to train models. With a private model, the information never leaves your perimeter. If a hybrid setup uses an external service, what information may leave and under what conditions is agreed in writing.

Where should a company start with AI?

With a concrete, measurable problem, not with the tool. The usual approach is to pick a repetitive process that eats up team hours today —document entry, answering inquiries, building reports— and solve it with a focused pilot. If it delivers, it scales. To identify that first case we offer a free assessment meeting.

What is the difference between using ChatGPT and a custom AI implementation?

Public tools are useful for individual tasks, but they don’t know your systems, can’t act on them, and process information on third-party servers. A custom implementation integrates with your ERP, CRM, databases or documentation, works with defined permissions, logs every action and can run in a private environment.

Do I need to buy servers or GPUs to have private AI?

Not necessarily. It depends on usage volume, the size of the model you need and how sensitive the data is. Sometimes owning the hardware makes sense; other times a dedicated private cloud or a hybrid setup is better. We size it during the assessment, before you have to invest.

How do you stay compliant with data protection law when using AI?

The key is knowing what personal data the solution processes, where it is processed and who has access. That is why we design every implementation with documented data processing, controlled access and the option to run everything inside your infrastructure —whether the framework is Argentina’s Law 25,326 or the GDPR. Our Audit, Risk and Compliance service also extends IT control assessments to the AI models your company adopts.

How much does AI implementation cost and how long does it take?

It depends on the use case, the state of your data and where the solution has to run. That is why we don’t sell fixed packages: after the assessment we give you the pilot’s scope, timeline and cost in writing, together with the metric that will define whether it worked.

Do you work with companies outside Argentina?

Yes. We are based in Buenos Aires and work with companies in any country: Latin America, Europe or wherever your operation is. Most of the implementation is done remotely and, when the project requires it, we travel for on-site work, as in recent projects in Germany. We work in English and Spanish.
