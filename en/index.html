About us

AI implemented by those who know the technical groundwork

Everyone offers AI today. Few know where it will run, what data it touches, and what risks it opens. We come from the backstage of the digital world: over two decades sustaining data centers, complex networks, and critical infrastructures that cannot fail.

That is why our AI is not a plugin you install: it is a layer that demands order, security, and solid infrastructure. Automation, agents, and private models built on deep knowledge of your systems — with privacy, control, and efficiency.

We are the technical partner that connects the potential of AI with the security of your infrastructure. Your competitive advantage: real, measurable, and secure.

Track record

We grew with every wave of technology, not just the latest one

We were born almost alongside the internet in Argentina and changed with every shift: broadband, virtualization, cloud, data. Today we lean heavily on AI — and we can put it into production because we have been operating the infrastructure it runs on all along.

  • 1999Born with the internet

    Argentina was connecting over dial-up. Networks, servers and the first corporate websites.

  • 2004Broadband

    ADSL brings always-on connectivity: in-house mail, remote access, and the first firewall that is genuinely needed.

  • 2009Virtualization

    One physical server becomes ten virtual ones. We redesigned entire infrastructures back when it was still a bet, not the norm.

  • 2014Cloud and mobility

    Data leaves the building and the phone becomes a workstation. We ran migrations and off-site backup years before the cloud was the obvious choice for a data center.

  • 2019Data and automation

    Before anyone talked about AI we were already integrating systems and putting data in order. That groundwork is what makes the rest possible today.

  • TodayApplied AI

    Agents and private models running on infrastructure we operate ourselves. AI is the new tool; the IT that holds it up we have been doing for 25 years.
