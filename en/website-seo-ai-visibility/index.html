FAQ

What people ask us about websites and visibility

Why doesn’t my website show up on Google?

The three most common causes: Google hasn’t indexed it properly, the site has no content answering what your customers search for, or there are no external signals —such as a Business Profile or links from other sites— telling Google your business is relevant. Google Search Console shows exactly which problem it is, and it’s the first thing we check in the audit.

What is AEO and how do I show up in ChatGPT?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is optimizing for answer engines: assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity that give a direct answer instead of a list of links. For them to mention you, your website has to be readable by their crawlers, explain clearly what you do and where, answer specific questions and include structured data. Nobody can guarantee an assistant will cite you, but a well-built website greatly improves the odds.

What does the website audit include?

We review Google indexing, which searches you appear for and in what position, speed and mobile experience, content, structured data, readability for AI assistants, your Google Business Profile and whether visits turn into inquiries. You get a report with problems ranked by priority and what to do about each one.

How long until I see results?

Technical fixes and Google registration show results in days or weeks. Climbing in competitive searches usually takes months, depending on your trade, your area and how much competition you have. That is why we measure with Search Console from the start: so you see progress in data, not promises.

Do you guarantee the top spot on Google?

No, and be wary of anyone who does: nobody controls Google’s results or AI answers. What we do is fix everything that depends on your website, target the searches that actually bring customers and show you with data how it evolves.

If I have Instagram and Google Maps, do I need a website?

Social media and a Business Profile help, but you don’t control them, and Google and AI assistants read them only partially. Your own website is where you explain in detail what you do and where, and answer your customers’ questions; it’s the source search engines and AI rely on most to decide whom to show.

How much does it cost?

It depends on whether we start from an existing website or from scratch, and how many sections and services it needs to cover. After the assessment we send you a concrete written proposal, with no obligation.
